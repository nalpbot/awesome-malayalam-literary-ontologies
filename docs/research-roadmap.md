# MALO-KG Research Roadmap

## From Malayalam Literary Ontology to an AI-Ready Literary Knowledge Graph

This project explores the development of a machine-actionable
knowledge representation framework for Malayalam literary studies.

The long-term objective is to connect Malayalam literary texts,
metadata, ontologies, knowledge graphs, NLP systems and large
language models.

## Research Architecture

Computational Literary Scholarship

## Research Questions

1. How can literary entities and relationships in Malayalam texts
   be represented using formal ontologies?

2. Can NLP and large language models automatically populate a
   Malayalam literary knowledge graph?

3. Can ontology-grounded retrieval improve the factuality and
   interpretability of LLM-based literary analysis?

4. How can literary history, themes, characters, places,
   movements and translations be represented computationally?

5. Can the framework support multilingual and comparative
   literary research?

## Proposed Development Phases

### Phase 1 — Ontology
Extend MALO with literary entities, relations and temporal concepts.

### Phase 2 — Corpus
Develop a machine-readable collection of Malayalam literary
metadata and selected textual material.

### Phase 3 — NLP
Investigate named-entity recognition, relation extraction,
event extraction and literary entity linking.

### Phase 4 — Knowledge Graph
Represent extracted information as a literary knowledge graph.

### Phase 5 — Graph + LLM
Develop ontology-grounded retrieval-augmented generation for
scholarly questions about Malayalam literature.

### Phase 6 — Scholarly Applications
Apply the framework to literary history, translation,
intertextuality, migration, environment and cultural memory.

## Long-Term Goal

The project aims to develop an open research infrastructure for
AI-assisted and interpretable computational study of Malayalam
